At a glance

Countr is a shop-management product operated by Countr Technology Pvt. Ltd. This policy explains what information may be handled through our website, application and related services, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

This policy is intended to be read together with the Terms & Conditions. It should be updated whenever the actual product, vendors, data flows or legal requirements change.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Countr may handle the following categories of information:

Account and shop information

  • Name or business name.
  • Mobile number, email address or other account identifiers you provide.
  • Shop name, business type, address or other shop details you choose to enter.
  • Login, authentication and account-status information.

Shop and transaction information

  • Product names, prices, quantities and inventory information.
  • Sales, purchases, expenses, payment records and related transaction information.
  • Khata entries, customer names, balances and transaction notes that you choose to record.
  • Reports, settings and other information created through your use of the service.

Device and technical information

  • Device type, operating system, app version and technical identifiers.
  • IP address, timestamps, diagnostic information and logs where collected by the service or infrastructure.
  • Information about crashes, performance and security events.

Information you provide when contacting us

If you contact support, we may receive the information you include in your message, attachments, screenshots and related correspondence.

2. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Provide, operate and maintain Countr and its features.
  • Create and manage accounts, shops and user access.
  • Record and display Khata, inventory, sales and other information at your direction.
  • Provide voice-enabled or manual workflows that you request.
  • Synchronise information where the product supports synchronisation.
  • Diagnose errors, improve reliability and develop product functionality.
  • Detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents or unlawful activity.
  • Communicate with you about your account, service changes, support requests and important notices.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory or governmental requirements.

We do not treat your shop data as permission to use it for unrelated purposes. Any new material use should be reflected in the applicable notice or this policy where required.

3. Voice commands and audio

Countr includes voice-based interaction. The exact handling of voice input depends on the implementation and configuration of the feature you use.

Where voice input is processed, it may be used to understand a command and convert it into an action such as a Khata entry, sale, inventory update or report request. Do not assume that a voice recording is stored or deleted immediately unless the product documentation specifically says so.

Before production launch, this section should be aligned with your actual speech-to-text provider, whether raw audio is retained, processing location, retention period and whether audio is sent to any third-party service.

4. When information may be shared

We may share or provide access to information only where reasonably necessary to operate the service, protect users, comply with law or perform a function you request.

Service providers

We may use third-party providers for infrastructure, hosting, databases, analytics, communications, authentication, payments, notifications, speech processing or other technical services. Such providers should receive only the access necessary for their role and should be covered by appropriate contractual or technical controls.

Legal and safety purposes

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, valid legal process, court or governmental order, or where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents or protect rights and safety.

Business changes

If Countr or relevant business assets are involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring or sale, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law and appropriate notice where required.

We do not sell your personal data as a product.

5. Storage, localisation and retention

Countr may use infrastructure located in India or other jurisdictions depending on the services and vendors used. Where information is processed outside India, we will handle it in accordance with applicable law and the commitments stated in our notices.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide the service, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, protect security, or comply with legal obligations.

Actual retention periods can differ by data type. Production documentation should specify retention schedules for account data, transaction records, logs, backups and support records.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information we handle. Depending on the system component, these may include access controls, authentication, logging, encryption in transit, secure infrastructure configuration, backups and monitoring.

No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting passwords, device access and account credentials, and for promptly reporting suspected unauthorised access.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and your role in relation to the information, you may have rights relating to personal data, including access to information, correction, withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis, deletion/erasure where applicable, grievance redressal and other rights provided by law.

You can also request account assistance, correction of inaccurate information or deletion of information where the product and applicable legal obligations permit it.

For requests, use the contact method listed in the Contact section. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Children's information

Countr is intended for businesses and their authorised users and is not designed as a service for children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating a business account. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, contact us so we can review the situation.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, security, preferences and, where enabled, analytics. The actual cookies and tracking technologies used should be listed in a cookie notice or consent mechanism where required.

10. Third-party links and services

Countr may contain links to third-party websites, app stores, payment services or other services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Review those policies before providing information to third parties.

11. International processing

Some service providers may process information outside the country where you are located. Where this occurs, Countr will apply the safeguards required by applicable law and the relevant service arrangements.

12. Data protection framework

Countr operates in India and will work toward applicable data-protection obligations relevant to the services it provides.
